Carpet, Upholstery & Mattress Cleaning

Specialist fabric-care services quoted around the item, material and condition - carpets, sofas, upholstery and mattresses.

Request a Free Cleaning Quote Book a Site Visit

Who this service is for

Homes, rentals, offices and landlords who need carpets, sofas, upholstery or mattresses refreshed, deodorised or stain-treated.

What is typically included

  • Hot water extraction deep clean
  • Stain treatment where safe for the fabric
  • Deodorising and freshening
  • Fast-drying process
  • Mattress sanitisation
  • Sofa and upholstery cleaning by item

Optional extras

  • Whole-house carpet cleaning
  • Pet odour treatment
  • Hard floor care
  • Fabric protection

Not included unless agreed

  • Flood damage restoration
  • Repairs or re-dyeing of fabrics
  • Furniture moving beyond safe lifting

Cleaning process

  1. Request a quote - tell us about your property or premises.
  2. We confirm scope - price and timing agreed in writing within 24 hours.
  3. We clean - a vetted, insured team arrives with eco-friendly products.
  4. You review - report any concerns within 24 hours and we will put it right.

How quotes work

Fabric-care quotes are based on the number of items, material, condition and access. Photos help us confirm scope.

Before arrival

Clear small items from surfaces, make sure power and water are accessible, and let us know about pets, allergies and particular stains.
