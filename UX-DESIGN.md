# UX design record

## Project goal

Bright Scrub Cleans is a quote-led cleaning service website for households,
landlords, tenants, offices and small commercial teams across the West
Midlands. The primary user task is to understand the service, trust the
business and request a quote with the least possible friction.

## Audience and user stories

- As a homeowner, I want to compare domestic cleaning options so I can choose
  the right service for my property.
- As a tenant or landlord, I want to find end-of-tenancy information quickly
  so I can plan a clean before a handover.
- As an office manager, I want to see commercial coverage and contact details
  so I can request a reliable quote.
- As a prospective customer, I want to contact the business by form, phone or
  WhatsApp so I can use the channel I trust.

## Five-plane decisions

1. **Strategy:** build trust, explain the offer clearly and generate qualified
   quote requests.
2. **Scope:** service pages, location pages, pricing guidance, FAQs, contact
   form, legal information and deployment documentation.
3. **Structure:** task-led navigation separates services, coverage, pricing,
   about and contact. Calls to action are repeated at natural decision points.
4. **Skeleton:** responsive cards, clear headings, labelled form fields,
   breadcrumb context and a mobile call/quote bar support scanning and task
   completion.
5. **Surface:** navy, teal and sky-blue brand colours provide contrast and a
   consistent visual hierarchy; Lexend headings and Source Sans body text
   support readability.

## Accessibility decisions

- Semantic landmarks, one page-level `h1`, ordered headings and skip links.
- Visible labels, native validation, a required consent control and a live
  status region for form feedback.
- Keyboard-operable navigation, FAQ buttons and gallery lightbox.
- Reduced-motion handling for counters and smooth scrolling.
- Descriptive image alternative text and `aria-expanded`/`aria-controls` for
  interactive components.
